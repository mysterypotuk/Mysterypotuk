Last updated: May 2026
1. Eligibility
You must be 18 years of age or older to participate in any game on Mystery Pot UK. By entering you confirm you are of legal age and that participation is legal in your country of residence.

All games on Mystery Pot UK require an element of skill to qualify. Games are not pure chance. The skill element ensures compliance with UK competition law. By playing you acknowledge the skill requirement.
3. Entry & Payment
• All games cost £1 per play
• Payments are processed securely via Stripe or PayPal
• All payments are non-refundable once an entry is confirmed
• Wallet deposits are non-refundable except in the case of technical error
4. The Draw
• The weekly draw takes place every Saturday at 8PM
• All tickets from all games enter the same weekly draw
• The winner is selected randomly from all valid paid tickets
• The host reserves the right to delay the draw in exceptional circumstances
• The draw result is final and binding
5. Prize & Payment
• The winner will be contacted via their stated social media platform
• Winners must claim their prize within 48 hours of being notified
• Unclaimed prizes will result in a redraw
• Prizes are paid within 24 hours of claiming
• Instant win prizes are paid within 24 hours via PayPal or bank transfer
• Mystery Pot UK reserves the right to verify winner identity before payment
6. Wallet
• Wallet funds can be used to play any game
• Minimum withdrawal: £2
• You must play at least 1 game before withdrawing
• Minimum £1 must remain in wallet after withdrawal
• Maximum 1 withdrawal request per 24 hours
• Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours via PayPal or bank transfer
7. Referrals
• Referral tickets are awarded when a friend registers and pays to play
• Referral tickets cannot be withdrawn — draw tickets only
• Mystery Pot UK reserves the right to remove referral bonuses if abuse is detected
8. Fair Play
• Multiple accounts are not permitted
• Any attempt to cheat or manipulate games will result in disqualification
• Mystery Pot UK reserves the right to refuse entry to anyone
• The host decision is final in all matters
9. Privacy
• Your username and platform are used solely to identify you as a winner
• Payment details are used only to process your withdrawal
• We do not share your data with third parties
• Bank details are deleted after withdrawal is processed
